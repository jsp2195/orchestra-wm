# I24-MSD short-horizon protocol v1

Preregistered before parsing held-out trajectories or examining model performance. This is a NEW dataset/protocol, not a replacement for continuous-I24 or Phase-2 criteria. Acquisition currently has authentic Harvard metadata; semantic schema and model results are not yet verified. All choices below are prospective; any necessary implementation deviation must be recorded before test scoring.

## Timing and population

Use the source-defined observation cutoff if provided. Otherwise use the first 2 seconds as context. Determine native dt/count from real records, preserve actual timestamps, and use a uniform causal grid only if supported by source timing. Evaluate horizons 1, 2, 4, 6 physical seconds ONLY where recorded future support permits. Primary horizon is 4 seconds; if no eligible support exists, the primary comparison is UNTESTED, not moved to a favorable horizon. No 10/20/60-second metrics, concatenated clips, or traffic-wave onset claims from nine-second scenes. Missing valid states remain masked, never future-interpolated into observations. Backward derivatives only when source velocities are missing; units/heading/map transforms require evidence.

Micro metrics use the historically observed cohort, with separate target existence, observed/detected and padded masks. Entrants after cutoff are counted in source QC but excluded from fixed-cohort forecasts; do not disclose their IDs to initialization. Open-system/inflow modeling is secondary and unvalidated unless separately implemented. Reconstructed offline source trajectories may themselves use future imagery; disclose this even with causal model inputs.

## Split and leakage controls

Preserve source partitions if present. Never infer a partition merely from filenames. Group by verified recording/session or origin IDs where available. Reject duplicate content across partitions and inspect scenario/track/time overlaps within a recording. If there are no source partitions, allocate whole independent origin groups by deterministic hash (seed 7301), approximately 60/20/20. If only one session exists, use buffered chronological blocks only when original time support can be recovered; otherwise deterministic scenario groups with near-duplicate/overlap guards are a provisional pilot, NOT independent-day generalization. If overlap cannot be excluded, do not claim a leak-free scientific comparison. Save immutable source/split manifests before training.

## Matched models and budgets

Pilot seed 101, fixed final checkpoint, at most 96 scenes and 32 agents/scene; 500 optimizer updates per learned variant, 2 CPU threads, 0 workers, total training budget 1800 s. Use the existing compact recurrent graph stochastic model (48 hidden, 6 latent), with the minimum evidence-required map/coordinate adaptation. No new action-conditioning or synthetic advisory labels. A 20-update real smoke is engineering evidence only. Research seeds 101/202/303 remain gated on pilot QC, validation and resource review; never automatically execute.

Compare persistence, constant velocity, independent learned dynamics, deterministic interaction-aware dynamics and full stochastic ORCHESTRA on identical inputs, splits, masks, horizons and targets. Train with autonomous prior rollouts and a proper stochastic objective; posterior target inputs may occur only inside the training loss and may never enter recurrent imagination states. Set the training unroll to up to 2 s at verified dt; validation uses autonomous predictions. Record actual updates, parameters, curves, checkpoint/data/source hashes and runtime. No test-based tuning or seed selection. Macro-only/micro–macro ablations require verified static lane geometry, observation footprint and meaningful population coverage; otherwise report N/A rather than invented lanes or unbiased density.

## Primary comparison and metrics

Primary architecture question: full stochastic versus independent learned predictor, 4-second joint position energy score, on the same historically observed agents and existing future targets. Use the joint vector of agent XY positions, Euclidean energy score divided by sqrt(number of valid coordinates), average scenarios equally. Report paired reduction (independent minus full), ADE/FDE and speed error alongside it; avoid best-of-K as a primary score. Primary support requires a positive lower 95% paired cluster-bootstrap bound and at least five independently identified source groups. With fewer groups, report the effect/interval as provisional and mark confirmatory support UNTESTED. 2000 bootstrap draws, seed 7103; pilot training uncertainty is unmeasured with one seed. Do not treat short related scenes as independent recordings.

Secondary: ADE/FDE/speed/heading (or lane error only with actual lane mapping), horizon drift, oriented overlap when headings/dimensions are known, verified road-boundary violations, 16-sample joint diversity, marginal 90% coverage/width, energy score and marginal CRPS. Keep denominators and skipped cases. Do not clip trajectories after generation to improve physics scores. Boundary/macro metrics are N/A when geometry or coverage cannot establish them.

Same checkpoint and scenarios: full graph versus disabled graph; intact memory versus reset inference; dense versus seeded 50% visible tracks and final-half-context outage. Recompute inputs from visible history only. These are EMULATED roadside sensing, not observed camera failures. No future values, target-validity masks, future rosters or incident labels may initialize predictions.

## Prior work, claims and showcase

Jayawardana et al., *Noise-Aware Generative Microscopic Traffic Simulation* (2025), arXiv:2508.07453, already adapts SMART with noise-aware losses to I24-MSD and compares IDM/CV. Basic generation from this release is not novel. Their likelihood/Sim Agents results are not comparable to our position-energy pilot without matched splits, noise treatment, context and metrics. Do not claim a benchmark win from incompatible published numbers. The proposed project contribution is structured partial-observation dynamics and residual interaction/micro-information value; it remains a hypothesis until measured.

A real showcase must use exact saved autonomous checkpoint arrays, source DOI/scenario IDs, real units/cutoff and checkpoint/source hashes. Revealed future truth is evaluation-only, never an imagined trajectory. Require a replay/leakage test. No authentic arrays means no REAL demo. Cavnue transfer remains interface-only, subject to authorized tracks, clocks/calibration, geometry/coverage, identity continuity, confidence and use permissions. Long-range waves, cross-corridor transfer and causal AV control remain UNTESTED.
